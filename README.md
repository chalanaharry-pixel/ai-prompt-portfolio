This repository contains AI Prompt Portfolios for four major business functions:

📈 Sales — AI-based Lead Scoring & Sales Forecasting
📣 Marketing — AI-based Personalized Marketing & Customer Segmentation
👥 Human Resources — AI-based Employee Attrition Prediction
💰 Finance — AI-based Loan Default Prediction

Each portfolio contains 25 prompts, progressing from basic and broad questions to advanced business and AI/ML analysis.

🧩 Prompt Structure

Each portfolio contains 25 prompts divided into 5 tiers:

Tier 1 — Broad Exploration

Introduces AI and its general applications in the business function.

Tier 2 — Industry / Domain Focus

Narrows the topic and introduces specific business areas and professional roles.

Tier 3 — Specific Business Problem

Focuses on a particular business problem and asks for structured analysis.

Tier 4 — Solution Design & Critical Evaluation

Introduces:

Solution architecture
Real-world examples
Reliable sources
Risk analysis
Ethics
Responsible AI
Tier 5 — Strategic & Expert-Level Synthesis

Covers:

Executive summaries
Self-verification
Implementation roadmaps
Stress testing
Business cases

This progression is designed so that each prompt becomes more specific and advanced than the previous level.

🎯 Purpose of the Portfolio

The purpose of this project is to demonstrate how prompt engineering can be used for AI/ML business research.

The prompts progressively introduce techniques such as:

Role assignment
Structured outputs
Real-world examples
Source requirements
Risk and ethics evaluation
Self-verification
Strategic analysis
